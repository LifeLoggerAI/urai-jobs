# Captured Reality Gaussian producer / consumer source compatibility

The Jobs Gaussian packager previously accepted positions up to 1e9 and scales up to 1e6, including positive scales below 1e-7 and all-transparent records. Spatial's actual streaming decoder rejects positions above 1e7, scales outside [1e-7, 1e4], and outputs with no visible points. A successful reconstruction could therefore produce immutable bytes that its runtime consumer refuses.

The producer now validates its Float32 output scales against the consumer's existing safety envelope and rejects an entirely transparent output. This changes no visual acceptance or release gate. The regression executes the real producer and the exact unmodified Spatial streaming/record validator from commit 9f59cd94a24e362a53b5ebf0c12eab986e207a6f with synthetic PLY and streamed Splat bytes. The fixture SHA256 is fcb01d265fe5e703e103708954f020720bd1d269bfa2668f112413bb37a58eee and is asserted before execution. Nine groups cover successful byte-preserving streams and each incompatible output boundary. Future consumer changes require a deliberate fixture refresh and compatibility review.
