This successor scans ordered owner pages of 100 and processes at most 10 pages per delivery, with an additional 40-second job-loop budget. A mid-page failure retains the previous cursor; durable completed job identities are skipped while pending identities are retried. It does not acknowledge global completion or schedule unapproved external work.
